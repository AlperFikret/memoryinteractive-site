TREEFALL

A roguelike tower defense where a living tree carries a tower of living weapons, drawn like a 1930s cartoon and staged like a paper theatre.

The show

Build a tower one floor at a time, and keep it standing.

Every floor of the tower is a weapon with a face: a minigun, a beehive, a flamethrower. Floors buff the floors next to them, two legendary neighbours fuse into an evolved weapon, and every boss you beat leaves a curse that makes the next act harder and the score bigger.

Why it plays differently

Three decisions in every camp

Stack and link

Floor order matters. Matching neighbours form pairs such as Oiled Flame or Charged Beam, and the non-firing Foreman floor powers the weapons above and below it.

Fuse

Two adjacent Legendary floors can fuse into one of ten evolutions, freeing a slot and changing how the weapon fires.

Take the curse

After each boss you must pick a curse. Rarer curses hit harder and pay more score, so record runs are deliberate risks.

Programme

Hard mode: three acts, then endless

Act I · waves 1–16

The Grove

Slimes, shields and splitters. Learn to build.

Act II · waves 17–32

The Pale Desert

Sand gulpers and ash flies. Ash Shell climbs out of a rift at wave 32.

Act III · waves 33–48

The Black Marsh

Leeches and lantern bugs. Bog Mother rises at wave 48; endless follows.
